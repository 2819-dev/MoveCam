Get MoveCam

Free to download. Updates install themselves.

Mac

The native app uses Apple's body tracking for the most accurate controls. macOS 14 or later.

  1. Download MoveCam.dmg and drag MoveCam to Applications.
  2. The first time, right-click MoveCam → Open → Open. (On macOS 15: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.)
  3. Allow camera access when asked.
Download for Mac

iPad

MoveCam runs as a full-screen app from your Home Screen — no App Store needed. iPadOS 16.4 or later.

  1. On your iPad, open movecam in Safari and tap Play now.
  2. Tap the Share button ⬆︎ then Add to Home Screen → Add.
  3. Open MoveCam from your Home Screen, tap Start and allow the camera.
  4. Stand the iPad up in landscape (a case stand works great) and step back.
Open MoveCam

Also works in Chrome, Edge and Safari on any computer with a webcam: play in your browser.
