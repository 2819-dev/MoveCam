How it works

Prop up your Mac or iPad, step back, and play.

1

Step back

Stand about 2 m (6 ft) away so the camera sees you from head to hips. The frame turns green when you're in the spot.

2

Pick a game

Swing an arm out to the side to browse. Raise a hand to play — or just tap.

3

Move!

Jump, duck, step and swing. Both hands up pauses. Body tracking runs on your device; video never leaves it.
